# Promo v6 — VO script (timings, direction, booth notes)

**The spoken copy lives in `2026-08-31-v6-vo-elevenlabs.txt` and ONLY there.** This file carries
timing and direction. Two files holding the same sentences is how they drift.

Read length: **~215 words → 68–72s** at an unhurried pace. Longer than v5 (61.7s) on purpose — v5
was tight because it was a scroll; v6 holds on charts and figures and needs the air.

## Direction

Unhurried, low, close to the mic. This is a person telling another person something useful, not an
announcer. **Land on the nouns, not the adjectives** — "Thursday afternoons", "four thousand
dollars", "a thousand pieces of coaching". Never sell the word "intelligence".

Two beats need a deliberate pause before them:
- before *"Then the part nobody else does."* — a full beat of silence; this is the turn.
- before the final *"It opens when the salon is quiet"* — the film should have already cut to the
  wordmark.

## Four defects from v5 this read is cut against

1. **The end impact is gone.** `promo/src/shots/v5/sfx.ts:52` fires `impact-deep-whoosh.mp3` at
   volume **0.55** — the loudest cue in the film by 60%, with a riser stacked under it at 0.34. Both
   come out. The sign-off lands on the wordmark and the last line of the read, not on a bang.
2. **The UVALUX logo appears.** `public/brand/uvalux-logo.png` and `uvalux-logo-4x.png` are on disk
   and referenced by nothing in the v5 film. The outro carries the lockup.
3. **Shots are composed, not scrolled.** v5 answered "just show the app" by stacking page strips and
   running a camera down them (`shots/v5/AppScroll.tsx`) — which reads as somebody scrolling a
   website. **48 element cutouts already exist** in `promo/public/textures/v5/` and the cut uses
   almost none of them. v6 frames them: push-ins, parallax, held figures, beat-synced cuts.
4. **Charts carry the open.** More figures, earlier. Beat 3 exists purely to put a moving chart on
   screen inside the first twenty seconds.
