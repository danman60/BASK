## What the re-time broke, and what was done about it
`FigurePlate`'s moves were FIXED-LENGTH — 90f for push and drift, 30f for settle —
and did not scale with the shot. Stretching `method` from 365f to 619f would have
left its plate frozen for 17.6 of its 20.6 seconds. Two fixes:

1. **`moveFrames` on FigurePlate.** Every shot now sizes its plate move to the beat
   it actually has. Nothing is frozen; a longer beat gets a slower move, which is
   the correct reading of a slower line.
2. **`method` travels instead of sitting.** Its copy grew from 12.2s to 20.6s over
   the SAME frame, so a single held plate could not carry it. `citation.png` is a
   full drill-down page — insight, chart, the records table, then "What this drew
   on" with one claim open to the quote. The shot now scales it up and pans down
   it, which is literally the journey the line describes ("it goes and finds where
   that advice came from ... the words somebody actually said on a stage"). It also
   makes that page legible for the first time; fitting a 740x1350 page inside a
   1080 frame rendered its body text at roughly nine pixels.

Two internal CUTs were re-pinned to their own silences rather than scaled:
`chart` 200 -> 196 (lands on "Traffic never moved"), `action` 150 -> 270 (lands on
"You read it, you change what you want").

`A12Calls` scrolls 0 -> 340 instead of 0 -> 170. At the new 264f its old range moved
0.64 page-px per frame, which reads as a frozen screenshot for 8.8 seconds. 340 is
inside the page (calls pageH 1588, viewport 871 page-px at zoom 1.24) and keeps the
`Spot` highlight in frame to the last frame.
