Bask · for the salon

Your business, already understood.

Bask watches revenue, memberships, retail, capacity, and inventory continuously, tells you what needs attention each morning, and turns every insight into a one-click action — a campaign, a message, an order, a staff challenge.

Nobody runs a report. Nobody opens a spreadsheet. The software does the analysis overnight and writes it in plain English: what changed, why it matters, and what to do about it.

Daybreak — the daily narrative brief, and the hero feature. “Check your Daybreak” is a habit you can say out loud.

Six destinations. Never a seventh.

TodayDaybreak brief · action queue · live pulse
The FloorRoom board · schedule · check-in · POS
CustomersProfiles · memberships · segments
MarketingStudio · campaigns · calendar
InventoryStock · forecasts · UVALUX ordering
InsightsAnalytics · Peers benchmarks · staff performance
Compass · for UVALUX

See the network you already serve.

Compass converts consented operational signals into rep call lists, coaching targets, expansion candidates, and product demand forecasts — making UVALUX measurably better at its existing business while creating a recurring software line.

Same nervous system, opposite end. Everything Compass shows is a derivation, never a passthrough: business signals, never the salon’s customer list.

Call List is the hero. Network is the leadership view; Call List is the rep’s morning. Account detail is shared by both.

Five destinations

NetworkMap + health distribution of all participating salons
Call ListToday’s prioritized outreach, each with a reason and a suggested conversation
AccountsSalon roster; account detail = trends, signals, order history, coaching log
SignalsProduct and category demand, regional trends, expansion candidates
CoachingTargets, playbooks, measured outcomes
