# Letter Agreement, Phase 1

**UVALUX International Inc.** and **Stream Stage Productions Inc.**
Date: ________________

This letter sets out what we have agreed for Phase 1. It is short on purpose. The full detail sits
in the Phase 1 Proposal dated August 2026, which this letter refers to and does not replace.

---

## 1. What is being built

A proof of concept for the UVALUX Intelligence Platform: **Bask**, salon intelligence for the salon
owner, and **Compass**, the intelligence layer for UVALUX over the network of salons running it.

Phase 1 delivers:

- working software, with database-backed accounts and interactive interfaces
- a salon intelligence dashboard demonstrating opportunity detection and recommendations
- a UVALUX intelligence dashboard, the internal view of account signals and opportunities
- an initial analytics engine, deterministic business calculations and opportunity detectors
- an AI recommendation layer that turns findings into plain-language recommendations
- a UVALUX knowledge prototype, semantic ingestion and retrieval of selected UVALUX expertise
- a benchmarking prototype comparing participating salons against their peers
- an initial data-ingestion framework
- demonstration datasets sufficient to show the product end to end
- pilot testing with a small group of UVALUX-selected stakeholders where practical
- a Phase 2 roadmap

**Anything not on that list is Phase 2 scope, agreed separately.**

## 2. What it costs

**C$25,000 + HST**, in three payments:

| | |
|---|---|
| **C$10,000** | on kickoff and commencement of development |
| **C$10,000** | on a working end-to-end MVP, delivered together with the Phase 1 findings and the Phase 2 roadmap |
| **C$5,000** | on completion of the Phase 1 pilot build |

Reasonable hosting and development infrastructure during the MVP period are included, unless
unusually large external costs are approved in advance.

## 3. How long it takes

**Phase 1 development runs to December 1, 2026.**

That date assumes kickoff in the week of September 8. Kickoff happens when UVALUX has provided the
sample data export and named the pilot stakeholders. Both are needed before the build can start, so
the schedule does not run while we are waiting for them, and a later kickoff moves the end date by
the same number of days.

**Phase 2, if we go ahead, runs to February 1, 2027.**

Development milestones inside each phase are set at that phase's kickoff.

## 4. What each side does

**Stream Stage Productions Inc. leads** product design, software development, data-model
development, AI architecture, analytics implementation, testing, iteration, and deployment of the
MVP.

**UVALUX supports** with industry knowledge, available data, pilot-customer introductions, feedback,
subject-matter expertise, and relevant training and coaching material.

This is an independent-contractor engagement. It is deliberately simple while the opportunity is
being validated.

## 5. Who owns what

Stream Stage Productions Inc. owns the software, frameworks, code, reusable technology and
development methods created during Phase 1. That is the whole of the software, not only the parts
that are general purpose.

UVALUX owns its own data, and that is not a small carve-out: customer information, business data,
configuration, branding, content, proprietary training, product information and internal knowledge
are UVALUX's, during Phase 1 and after it. Nothing here gives Stream Stage Productions Inc. a claim
on any of it.

UVALUX receives the right to use the Phase 1 product internally, and with agreed pilot participants,
for evaluation and testing.

**If Stream Stage Productions Inc. stops work, if Daniel Abrahamson is unable to continue, or if
either party ends this agreement, UVALUX keeps the right to run the Phase 1 build for its own
salons, permanently, at no further cost.** That right is
non-exclusive and does not extend to reselling it, licensing it to others, or offering it as a
product. It exists so that UVALUX is never stranded by something happening to one person.

If Phase 1 becomes a broader commercial product, the parties will agree the commercial structure as
part of Phase 2 planning. That might be licensing, revenue share, exclusivity, equity, a joint
venture, an acquisition, or something else. **Nothing in Phase 1 commits either party to any of
them.**

## 6. Ending it

Either party may end Phase 1 in writing. Payments already earned at that point remain payable, and
payments not yet earned are not owed.
