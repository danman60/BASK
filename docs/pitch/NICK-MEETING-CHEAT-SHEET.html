Short, honest answers

Objection lines

Answer, verify understanding, return to discovery. Do not defend what is not built.

"Is this production software?"

No. It is a deployed interactive discovery build. It demonstrates proposed workflows for evaluation. Production work remains.

"Does it run T-Max or bed timers?"

Not today. The integration boundary is designed, but hardware needs bench verification before I promise compatibility.

"Are payments and messages real?"

No. Payment events, campaign publishing, and results are simulated in this build. Production providers are still a decision.

"Where is the mobile app?"

A native app is not built. What you are seeing on the phone is the responsive live web product.

"Who owns the data?"

The design gives the salon explicit sharing controls. Compass uses derived business signals, not customer names, contacts, or transactions. Legal and compliance work still needs to validate that design.

"How would migration work?"

Working direction is new salons first and concierge import later. I will not promise payment-token portability or a migration path before we verify the source systems.

"What does this cost?"

The current numbers are hypotheses, not a quote. First validate owner demand and choose the UVALUX structure, then price the real scope.

"What do you need from me?"

Your honest read, brand direction, introductions to 3 to 5 trusted owners, and a dated next step if the concept earns one.

Unknown answer

"I do not know yet. I will verify that and send you the answer by [day and time]." Write it down before moving on.

Trust protection

Red lines

Say less. Do not upgrade hypotheses into facts.

Do not claim
  • Native Bask or Compass mobile app exists.
  • Product is production-ready or pilot-ready.
  • T-Max or other hardware is certified or integrated.
  • Payments, messages, campaign results, or benchmark data are live.
  • Pricing or commercial structure is approved.
  • UVALUX branding or participation is approved.
  • Migration, privacy compliance, or payment-token portability is solved.
  • Unknown UVALUX customer counts or market figures are facts.
Do say
  • Polished interactive discovery build.
  • Responsive live web on phone.
  • Hardware designed as an integration boundary, verification pending.
  • Simulated events and staged data where relevant.
  • Working pricing and structure hypotheses.
  • Consent-gated product design, legal validation pending.
  • Unknowns get a named verification date.
