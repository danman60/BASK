## What I'm proposing to build

**A benchmarking and coaching layer for salons, fed by the data you already hold.**

Not a POS. Not booking. Not a room board. Those come out.

1. **Scoreboard.** A salon submits its numbers — or you plug in the data you already host — and gets
   back where it ranks. Percentile against the group, by category, month over month. Participants
   see themselves; non-participants see the anonymized industry line. The thing owners in California
   told you they'd love.
2. **Customer health.** Every customer scored and on one grid — who's loyal, who's slipping, who's
   gone. The lapsed ones come with a reach-out ready to send. At the desk, the staff member serving
   someone sees it's been 85 days and what that person usually buys.
3. **Bottle depletion.** You don't track how full their bottle is, but you track tans used, last
   purchase, and roughly half an ounce per tan. So we can compute it — and prompt the reorder before
   the customer thinks of it.
4. **Campaigns off the data.** One click from "Thursdays are soft" to the offer, the post and the
   text. Tuned to this industry and driven by that salon's own numbers, which is what makes it not
   look like everyone else's ChatGPT output.
5. **Coaching, systematized — delivered by you.** Elaine's coaching, Mike's numbers, Sarah's
   recordings become a knowledge base the product reasons over. It surfaces what to say. **You and
   Elaine stay the experts and the face.** People buy from people; the software just makes sure the
   right conversation happens more often.

Built to plug into other platforms through an API from day one, and built sector-agnostic — so when
Angie is knocking on spas, saunas and gyms, there's something to sell her.
