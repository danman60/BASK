## What I'm proposing to build

**A benchmarking, insight and coaching layer for salons, fed by data you already hold.**

Not a POS. Not booking. Not a room board. Those come out.

1. **The scoreboard.** A salon's numbers come back as a rank — percentile against the group, by
   category, month over month. Participants see themselves; non-participants see the anonymized
   industry line. The thing owners in California told you they'd love. **Units as well as dollars**,
   because "you sell 35 tanning lotion while the average is 22" is the version that lands.
2. **Customer health.** Every customer scored on one grid — loyal, slipping, gone. Lapsed ones come
   with a reach-out ready to send. At the desk, whoever is serving someone sees that it's been 85
   days and what that person usually buys.
3. **Bottle depletion.** You don't track how full the bottle is, but you track tans used, last
   purchase, and roughly half an ounce per tan — so it's computable. The reorder conversation happens
   before the customer thinks of it, and it cues the sister product when they've plateaued.
4. **Campaigns off the data.** One click from "Thursdays are soft" to the offer, the post and the
   text — tuned to this industry and driven by that salon's own numbers, which is what stops it
   looking like everyone else's ChatGPT output. **This replaces what HootSuite is doing for you now.**
5. **Coaching, systematized — delivered by you.** Elaine's coaching, Mike's numbers, Sarah's
   sessions become a knowledge base the product reasons over, and it surfaces what to say.
   **You and Elaine stay the experts and the face.** People buy from people; the software only makes
   sure the right conversation happens more often.

Built to plug into other platforms through an API from day one, and sector-agnostic — so when Angie
is knocking on spas, saunas and gyms, there's something to sell her.

## Roadmap beyond phase 3 — where this goes

Not commitments. The shape of the thing, so you can see whether it's worth owning.

- **The community.** You said it yourself — they come for the lotion, but *"the community's the
  biggest one."* A members-only space where owners post their numbers and ask each other, with the
  noise moderated out. Not Facebook groups run by two loud people. **I've already built this for
  another client** — feed, profiles, follow, algorithm — so it's a port, not a build. And what
  everyone asks about becomes its own signal.
- **One click to book a coaching call.** The bridge from an insight to a human — you or Elaine. It's
  how the software makes the coaching business bigger instead of competing with it.
- **Equipment payback modelling.** Your cocoon pitch, with evidence attached: *"salons like yours
  that added this modality hold members a month longer, so this machine pays back in N months."*
  This is the one I'd argue is the biggest commercial idea in the whole thing — it's a selling
  instrument for the highest-ticket item you sell, not a retention feature.
- **Staff coaching from real conversations.** Your Chaz story — bottling the pitch that works. It
  starts as data only: whose reach-outs land, whose closing ratio is what, no microphones. **The
  audio version stays off the table until counsel says otherwise**, and I'd rather leave it out than
  have it be the reason a salon says no.
- **Sector expansion.** Spas, saunas, gyms, wellness — Angie's territory. The build is
  sector-agnostic and API-pluggable from day one so this doesn't need a rewrite.
