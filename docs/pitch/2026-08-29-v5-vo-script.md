## Notes for the booth

- **Pause, don't rush, at three places:** after "what to do about it" (shot 1),
  after "Things to do today" (shot 3), and after "There's a second side to it"
  (shot 10). The picture holds for each.
- "A hundred and ninety-four thousand" is read long, not as digits.
- Nothing in the read names a feature the picture has not already shown.
- **The corpus claim is deliberately bounded.** The knowledge queue really holds 741
  claims mined from the UVALUX Expo 2026 training rooms, and 3 are decided — so the
  read says they are "reviewed one at a time" and does NOT say review gates what
  reaches a salon. What the salon-side cards genuinely carry is the method
  attribution in `packages/core/src/sources/experts.ts`, which traces each technique
  to the UVALUX advisory, de-identified (never the advisor's name — owner directive,
  2026-08-22).
- The UVALUX section is deliberately thin: the film says what Compass *is* and
  stops. Anything more belongs in the live demo.
