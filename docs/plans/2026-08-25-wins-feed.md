# Plan — the Wins Feed (Bask's social layer)

**Spec from Daniel, 2026-08-24 (voice).** Pattern lifted from Stageable's feed
(`~/projects/Stageable/shots/shotcraft-20260822/feed-desktop.png`): three columns — rail, card
feed, context rail.

## What it is, in his words

> *"like you log on and it looks like Instagram, but instead of social posts it's wins from other
> salons and what they did… especially show wins that occur from taking actions that Bask provides…
> 6 ways to grow your business on the left, with one click through to marketing and the LLM
> generation assets… gate it as a feature flag so we can turn it on and off."*

**This exists so that a salon owner logging in sees other salons winning with Bask's own
recommendations, and can start the same action in one click.**

## The thesis (Daniel, 2026-08-24)

> **"Our users are SOCIAL DRIVEN. Seeing others like them succeeding — and why — will drive them.
> Wins/posts should have comments, likes and DMs. Think between Instagram and LinkedIn."**

This is the product bet, and it changes the shape of the feature: engagement is not decoration on
top of a data feed, it IS the mechanism. An owner acts because a peer they respect did it and said
it worked, not because a dashboard ranked it. Every win therefore carries a real conversation.

**What that adds beyond a read-only feed:** likes (the cheapest signal, and the one that tells the
ranking engine which wins actually land), comments (where the "why" lives — the part the data can
never supply), and owner-to-owner DMs.

**LinkedIn not Instagram, on two axes:** identity is a business and its town, never a person and
never a photo; and the currency is a measured result, never a mood. Instagram is the borrowed
SHAPE — a scrollable feed of cards with light engagement — not the borrowed content.

**What this does NOT change:** the non-compete rule holds absolutely, including for DMs. Two
competing salons must never be able to reach each other through this product. Every social
affordance inherits the same filter as the feed.

## Four rules that make it different from a social network

1. **Wins, not posts.** A feed item is an ACTION Bask recommended + what the salon did + what the
   number did afterward. Not chat, not photos, not status updates. If it has no measured outcome it
   is not a win and does not belong in the feed.
2. **NON-COMPETING SALONS ONLY.** An owner must never see the shop down the road outperforming
   them. Geographic exclusion is a hard product rule, not a filter preference — it is what makes an
   owner willing to be in the feed at all. Default exclusion radius plus same-city exclusion.
3. **Closes the loop back to the engine.** Every win names the Bask action that produced it, so the
   feed doubles as evidence for the recommendation engine and as training data (see
   `summariseNetworkOutcomes` in `@bask/core/network/outcomes`).
4. **Feature-flagged.** The whole surface toggles off without touching routes or nav. Empty feed in
   a pilot of three salons reads as a dead product — the flag is how we avoid showing that.

## The leaderboard merges in here — and the non-compete rule is what makes it safe

Ranking already exists and is already wired: `apps/web/src/server/peers.ts` computes `percentile`,
`cohortMedian`, `cohortTopQuartile` and `winning` behind the consent gate, and `ScoreboardSection`
renders it at `/insights/peers`. **Do not rebuild any of that.** The merge is to bring a compact
standing into the feed's right rail, over the same non-competing cohort the feed uses.

**Why the two ideas belong together:** a leaderboard is normally hostile — an owner who sees they
are 47th of 60, or bottom quartile against the shop down the road, disengages or disputes it. The
non-compete filter removes exactly that objection. Ranked against salons you will never lose a
customer to, a standing stops being a threat and becomes a target. **The filter is what makes the
leaderboard socially possible, and the leaderboard is what gives the feed a reason to return
daily.**

Two fences carried over from the existing code, both deliberate:
- The codebase already refuses to rank STAFF — `StaffChallengeCard`: *"bars are progress toward a
  personal target, never a leaderboard rank"*, and `insights-data.ts:317`: *"staff view:
  coaching-framed, never a leaderboard."* Salon-vs-salon is a different question; staff ranking
  stays off.
- Show a band and a gap, never an ordinal position in a list. `PositionBand` (`top`/`above`/
  `below`/`bottom`) already exists for this. "You are in the top quarter for product per visit" is
  usable; "you are 47th" is not.

## Layout

| column | contents |
|---|---|
| left rail | **Six ways to grow your business** — ranked one-click actions, each routing into the existing marketing / campaign / LLM-generation flow |
| centre | the wins feed — cards, newest and most relevant first |
| right rail | **your standing** among non-competing salons — band + gap to median, never an ordinal. Plus the metric you currently lead on, so every owner has something they are winning at. |

## Decomposition

**Local (presentational leaves + pure logic):**
- `WinCard` — one salon's win. Props-only, every figure pre-formatted.
- `PeerStandingCard` — the compact rail standing. Reuses `PositionBand` and `CohortRow`.
- `GrowthRail` — the six actions. Props-only, click handlers passed in.
- `packages/core/src/network/wins.ts` — types + the non-compete filter + ranking. Pure.
- `packages/core/src/network/standing.ts` — standing over the non-competing cohort. Pure.
- `packages/core/src/flags.ts` — feature-flag reader. Pure.

**Supervisor (me):**
- The route, nav entry and page composition.
- **Engagement model** - likes, comments, DMs. New tables, and the hardest consent surface in the
  product: a DM channel between owners needs moderation, reporting, and the non-compete filter
  applied to the recipient list, not only to the feed.
- Persistence: `bask.network_win` table — needs an explicit go, shared CC&SS database.
- Consent: publishing a win is a NEW grant, separate from anonymous benchmarking. A win names a
  salon's town; `packages/core/consent` must gate it.
- Wiring the six actions into the real marketing/generation flow.

## Reuses, does not rebuild

`CommunityFeed` and `CommunityComposer` already exist (built, exported, unwired). `WinCard` is a
different object from `CommunityPost` — a win is measured and system-generated; a post is written by
a person. Both can live on the surface; do not collapse one into the other.

## Open
