The build price is fixed. Phase 1's C$25,000 is always paid in cash. Any value not paid in cash is carried by Stream Stage Productions Inc., not discounted.

Commercial share is economics, not access. It applies only to agreed downstream licensing. It is not automatically equity. Stream Stage Productions Inc. owns the software and UVALUX owns its data, as in the Phase 1 letter.

How the slider works: UVALUX's share runs from 5% to 95% in 1% steps. Cash = the minimum cash plus the share above 5%, as a fraction of 90%, times the rest of the build price, in $500 steps. The minimum cash is Phase 1's C$25,000, fixed in the Phase 1 letter. Share bought after the build is priced at least 50% above the rate fixed at signing.
