## What this script is

The film's thesis changed. It is no longer a tour of screens. It is one argument:

> Your salon already makes the data. Bask turns it into money-first opportunities, prepares the
> action, and you press one button. Then it shows you what the action made. And it listens where
> the money actually happens — the front desk.

The chain every beat serves: **data → intelligence → dollar opportunity → one-click action →
measured outcome**. Never pitch salon-management features (booking, POS, minutes) — the product's
buyer explicitly ruled that category out. The words "salon management" must not appear.

## The 10 beats

1. **Cold open — the owner's morning.** A quiet observation: you already know something is off;
   you just can't see where. One or two lines.
2. **The thesis.** Your data already knows. Bask reads it every night and comes back with a
   short list: N ways to grow your business today, each with a dollar figure. Say the word
   "intelligence" here.
3. **Opportunity one — evening retail.** Attachment fell from 21% to 14%; worth about $1,270 a
   month; the fix is a staff challenge, already prepared.
4. **One click.** The product's promise in one line: every recommendation opens into the thing
   that does it — the text, the email, the post, the challenge. Approve and it runs. Use the
   phrase "one click".
5. **Customer health.** Who is slipping before they are gone, and the call list that follows.
6. **Peers / analytics.** Where you stand against salons like yours, and what the gap is worth
   in dollars.
7. **The Front Desk Monitor.** The listener at the desk hears every sales conversation, scores
   the coachable moments, and finds what your best people do differently. Coaching, in the open,
   on the salon's terms. Say the word "Monitor".
8. **Proof.** Last month's action, measured: 5 new memberships, $375 a month, recurring. The
   software tells you what worked, not just what to do.
9. **The network.** UVALUX knowledge and consenting peers behind every recommendation — the
   coaching of the whole network, aimed at your salon. Consent stays the salon's switch.
10. **Sign-off.** One line, the promise: you don't need to read the data — press the button.
    Then the product name.

## Voice rules

- Grade-7 plain English. Short sentences. Money in the sentence wherever it fits.
- Honest: "worth testing" is allowed language; certainty is never faked.
- NEVER "built for UVALUX" or any phrase implying an official partnership exists.
- No feature-list narration; every line either advances the argument or is cut.
